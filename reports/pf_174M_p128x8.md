## Outcome
The larger pair track led the 64-dim one by ~0.03 TM at equal epochs throughout training (0.581 vs 0.525 at epoch 8, 0.602 vs 0.570 at 16), but part of that was its doubled step count at batch 64, and at the plateau the held-out advantage is 0.008 over the small track and 0.012 over no pair, with no-neighbour differences of 0.005-0.013. All within about one standard error. With ESM-2 conditioning on 80k proteins, pair capacity is not where the accuracy is. Coverage 100% at all 20 evaluations.

## What it changed
Closes the pair-size question at 80k/ESM-2: 64 -> 128 dims and 6 -> 8 blocks buys ~0.01 TM. Together with the two 64-dim ablations it says the pair track's value in this regime is convergence speed, and that accuracy gains have to come from the conditioner and the data, which is what the H200 main line does. Whether pair size matters more with ESMC-6B and 473k proteins is being measured directly by `pf_459M_p128x8_esmc_afdb` (ahead of its 64-dim predecessor by 0.03 at epoch 10). With this run stopped, the account is back to the eight H200s of that job, as requested.
