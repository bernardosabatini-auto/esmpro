## What it changed
Capacity helps the FAPE head, but 6x the parameters bought 0.02 TM. The 171M head (`h200_24L768`) did not add more. Together they say the single-track head is architecture-limited, not size-limited, which supports the switch to the latent flow model (0.60 on the same data, `reports/STATUS.md`).
