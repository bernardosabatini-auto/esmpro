Third independent confirmation that 459M on 80k proteins saturates at TM ~0.60 / ~70% correct folds, whatever the batch size or GPU. It also validated the memory rule in practice: batch 256 filled the H200 without OOM and reached the plateau in 5.6 h on one card.
