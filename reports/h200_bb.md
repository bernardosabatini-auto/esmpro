## Outcome
TM 0.478 / 46% at epoch 11, against the control's 0.477 / 45%: true frames alone neither helped nor hurt. The section-8 worry did not materialise as a failure. The measured floors explain why: a perfect latent decodes with 0.058 A bond scatter, giving a bb-FAPE floor of 0.131 versus 0.053 for Ca frames (`code/gate6_bbfape_floor.py`), but the head sits at 0.83, so frame noise was about 15% of the signal, not dominant. Without a penalty the N-CA scatter stayed at 0.31 A (the loss itself pulls it down only slowly). Coverage 100% throughout. Held-out (offset 1000, selection-free) score of `best_h200_bb.pt` (epoch 11): TM 0.468, TM>0.5 44%, TM>0.3 84%, RMSD 11.67 A, coverage 100/100. The FAPE control's best checkpoint scores 0.466 / 42% / 11.74 A on the same slice; the inherited checkpoint 0.420 / 29%.

## What it changed
It retires the section-8 caution as a hard rule: true frames are safe to use on decoded backbones at this error level. It also shows they are not a lever on their own. With `h200_bb_bond` and `h200_cabb_bond` it closes the loss-function axis for the FAPE head: Ca frames, true frames, and their sum all land on the control's curve.
