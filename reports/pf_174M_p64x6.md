## Outcome
Early on the pair track looked like a large lever: +0.11 TM over the no-pair twin at epoch 4 and +0.05 at epoch 8. The lead shrank steadily (+0.03 at 12, +0.01 at 24) and at the plateau the two models are indistinguishable on every slice (differences of 0.003-0.006, well inside noise). The pair track therefore accelerates learning of the sequence-to-latent map but, with ESM-2 conditioning on 80k proteins, does not change where it ends up. Coverage 100% at all 30 evaluations.

## What it changed
A null result for the 64-dim pair track as an *accuracy* lever on ESM-2/80k, and a positive one as a *convergence* lever (about 2x fewer epochs to any given TM, at 2.2x the step cost, i.e. roughly wall-clock neutral). The larger 128-dim x 8 track (`pf_174M_p128x8`) keeps a ~+0.03 lead at equal epochs and is the configuration carried into the H200 run `pf_459M_p128x8_esmc_afdb`; whether that lead survives to the plateau is the open question. The engineering that came out of this arm (bucketed residue-budget batching, compiled checkpointed pair blocks, the shared bias projection) is what made the 473k H200 runs affordable.
